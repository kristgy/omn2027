Welcome to OMN 2027

Premier International Forum on Micro & Nano Photonics

The International Conference on Optical MEMS and Nanophotonics (OMN) is the flagship international conference dedicated to micro-optical electro-mechanical systems and nano-scale photonic phenomena.

In June 2027, Stockholm welcomes the international scientific community to foster interdisciplinary discussions, unveil groundbreaking research, and establish strategic collaborations across optics, MEMS, and materials science.

  • Cutting-Edge Technology

    From tunable metasurfaces to silicon photonics and quantum sensors.

  • High-Impact Networking

    Connect with leading academic chairs, industry leaders, and emerging researchers.

  • White Nights of Stockholm

    Experience Scandinavia's vibrant midsummer light during conference evening events.

Scientific Scope

Conference Tracks & Topics

OMN 2027 invites original research contributions across a wide spectrum of micro-optics and nanophotonics fields.

Optical MEMS & Tunable Devices

Micro-mirrors, MEMS scanners, tunable filters, deformable mirrors, and optical switches for display, LiDAR, and telecom.

  • MEMS scanners & 3D beam steering
  • Adaptive micro-optics & microlenses
  • Optical actuators & piezo-MEMS

Nanophotonics & Metamaterials

Metasurfaces, plasmonics, photonic crystals, near-field optics, and subwavelength light manipulation.

  • Flat optics & dielectric metasurfaces
  • Plasmonic biosensors & cavities
  • Subwavelength light-matter interaction

Integrated & Silicon Photonics

Photonic integrated circuits (PICs), silicon & III-V integration, optical interconnects, and waveguide systems.

  • Heterogeneous III-V on silicon integration
  • Low-loss waveguide networks
  • On-chip lasers & high-speed modulators

Quantum & Nonlinear Optics

On-chip single-photon sources, quantum key distribution, frequency combs, and nonlinear nanophotonics.

  • Integrated quantum light sources
  • Micro-resonator frequency combs
  • Nonlinear metasurfaces & harmonic generation

Biophotonics & Medical Sensing

Lab-on-a-chip optical devices, point-of-care diagnostics, optofluidics, and biomedical imaging systems.

  • Optofluidic lab-on-a-chip platforms
  • Label-free optical biosensors
  • Endoscopic MEMS probes

Micro/Nano Fabrication & Packaging

Advanced lithography, 3D laser nanoprinting, wafer bonding, and micro-optical system integration packaging.

  • Two-photon direct laser writing
  • Wafer-level optical packaging
  • Novel optical materials & thin films
